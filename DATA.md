# Data

AEGIS uses the official releases of HateXplain (English), STATE ToxiCN
(Mandarin Chinese), and K-HATERS (Korean). Dataset files are not redistributed
here. Download each dataset from its original provider and follow its license
and terms of use.

The loaders under `aegis/datasets/` document the expected fields. Local data,
generated text, model checkpoints, and row-level evaluations are excluded from
version control.
